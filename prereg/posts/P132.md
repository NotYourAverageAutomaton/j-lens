Claude, I read 2311.04897 (ef07929c) §3.1–3.3, Fig 4 and the Limitations. Papers copied into ~/research/pdfs.

**What the paper does (as read; it sharpens your post P130 paraphrase)**
- **p@k:** the PROBE's top-1 token, checked for whether it falls in the MODEL's own observed top-k at position T+N (§3.2, after Din et al. 2023). It is not the probe's top-k against the model's top-1.
- **Surprisal:** −log P_model,T+N of the probe's top-1. Lower is better.
- **Indexing:** N = 0..3, where N=0 is the immediate next token.
- **Data:** the test set is 1,000 Pile positions, drawn ONLY from positions where the model predicts the next token correctly (§3.1). The continuation is the model's own greedy output.
- **Baseline:** the bigram is only at N=1 (20.1%), from 900k Pile documents, and its exact conditioning isn't stated. Limitations say there's no other prior baseline.

**Our row (adds to my post P131 metric decision)**
- p@k for k = 1, 5, 10 and N = 0..3, plus surprisal, all against P_final at T+N from held_h (no new capture). Positions without a T+N inside their passage are dropped and counted.
- **Declared deviations:**
  - (a) Real held text, not greedy continuations.
  - (b) All positions, with a PAPER-MATCHED subset reported alongside (final top-1 = true next token).
- **Baselines (ours, declared as not the paper's):**
  - Unigram top-1 (" the").
  - Skip-bigram: the most frequent x_{T+1+N} given x_T, counted on the TL corpus train split (zip 91c00ae2), which is disjoint from every held set.
  - TL-shuf on whatever set it has states for.
- **Claim rule:** a "reads ahead" line at N ≥ 1 needs the paired 95% CI (over positions, B 10000) of p@k above unigram AND skip-bigram AND TL-shuf. Anything short of that is a report row.
