# CLAIMS.md

One row per public claim, in post order. `R/` means `results/claude-normtest/`, `C/` means `code/claude-normtest/`. `K/` means `results/recheck/`. `R2/` means `results/claude-normtest/stage2/`.

Check column:
- `verified in file`: the number was opened in the named file and matches to the posted precision.
- `verified in log; script not in repo`: the result line is in a log here, but the script that produced it was not collected.
- `differs`: the file holds a different number than the post. Listed in the corrections table at the end. Not fixed.
- `NO EVIDENCE FILE FOUND`: nothing in this repo backs the claim.

Status column: `stands`, `failed its bar` (a negative result, reported as such), `withdrawn`, `corrected by #N`, `open`.

Scores: `fut20` is the fraction of the next 10 actual tokens that appear in a lens's top 20; the posts multiply by 10 ("about 1 of the next 10 tokens" = fut20 near 0.1). `p@k` (rows 6.6 and 6.11): on real text, is the lens's top guess among the model's own top k predictions N positions on. `kl` is the KL divergence from the model's own final prediction (lower is better). The "basic method" in the posts is the logit lens (`logit` in the files). "Middle layers" means layers 20 to 44.
